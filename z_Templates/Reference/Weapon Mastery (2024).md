> Quick reference for **2024 Weapon Mastery**. Condensed and paraphrased from the free SRD 5.2.

**Who and how:** Barbarians, Fighters, Paladins, Rangers and Rogues have Weapon Mastery — they pick a number of weapon kinds and can use those weapons' mastery property (other classes can get it with the *Weapon Master* feat). You need proficiency with the weapon. After a **Long Rest** you can swap one of your chosen weapon kinds for another.

## Mastery Properties

| Mastery | What it does | Weapons |
|---|---|---|
| **Cleave** | When you hit with a melee attack, make another melee attack against a second creature within 5 ft of the first and in your reach. On a hit it takes the weapon's damage without your ability modifier (unless negative). Once per turn. | Greataxe, Halberd |
| **Graze** | When your attack roll with this weapon **misses**, the target still takes damage equal to the ability modifier you used (same damage type). | Glaive, Greatsword |
| **Nick** | The extra attack of the **Light** property can be made as part of the Attack action instead of as a Bonus Action. Once per turn. | Dagger, Light Hammer, Scimitar, Sickle |
| **Push** | On a hit, push a Large or smaller creature up to 10 ft straight away from you. | Greatclub, Heavy Crossbow, Pike, Warhammer |
| **Sap** | On a hit, the target has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn. | Flail, Longsword, Mace, Morningstar, Spear, War Pick |
| **Slow** | On a hit that deals damage, reduce the target's Speed by 10 ft until the start of your next turn (doesn't stack). | Club, Javelin, Light Crossbow, Longbow, Sling, Whip |
| **Topple** | On a hit, the target makes a CON save (DC 8 + the ability modifier you attacked with + your Proficiency Bonus) or is knocked **Prone**. | Battleaxe, Lance, Maul, Quarterstaff, Trident |
| **Vex** | On a hit that deals damage, you have Advantage on your next attack roll against that creature before the end of your next turn. | Blowgun, Dart, Hand Crossbow, Handaxe, Rapier, Shortbow, Shortsword |
