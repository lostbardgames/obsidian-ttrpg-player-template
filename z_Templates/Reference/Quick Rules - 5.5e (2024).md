> Quick reference for the **2024 D&D 5.5e** rules. Shown on the Player Screen; pick the edition there. Condensed and paraphrased from the free SRD 5.2.

> [!column|3 no-t]
>
> > [!danger|no-t] **💀 Conditions**
> >
> > **Blinded** — Can't see; auto-fail checks that need sight. Attacks against you have Advantage; your attacks have Disadvantage.
> >
> > **Charmed** — Can't attack the charmer or target them with damaging effects. The charmer has Advantage on social checks against you.
> >
> > **Deafened** — Can't hear; auto-fail checks that need hearing.
> >
> > **Exhaustion** — Levels 1–6 (6 = death). D20 Tests are reduced by 2 × level; Speed is reduced by 5 ft × level. A Long Rest removes 1 level.
> >
> > **Frightened** — Disadvantage on checks and attacks while the source is in line of sight; can't willingly move closer to it.
> >
> > **Grappled** — Speed 0. Disadvantage on attacks against anyone but the grappler. The grappler can drag or carry you.
> >
> > **Incapacitated** — No actions, Bonus Actions or Reactions; Concentration is broken; can't speak. Disadvantage on Initiative if it applies when you roll.
> >
> > **Invisible** — Advantage on Initiative if invisible when you roll. Attacks against you have Disadvantage; your attacks have Advantage.
> >
> > **Paralyzed** — Incapacitated; Speed 0; auto-fail STR and DEX saves; attacks against you have Advantage; hits from within 5 ft are Critical Hits.
> >
> > **Petrified** — Turned to stone: Incapacitated, Speed 0, auto-fail STR/DEX saves, attacks against you have Advantage, Resistance to all damage, immune to Poisoned.
> >
> > **Poisoned** — Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.
> >
> > **Prone** — Crawl, or spend half your Speed to stand. Disadvantage on your attacks. Attacks against you have Advantage from within 5 ft, otherwise Disadvantage.
> >
> > **Restrained** — Speed 0; attacks against you have Advantage; your attacks have Disadvantage; Disadvantage on DEX saves.
> >
> > **Stunned** — Incapacitated; auto-fail STR and DEX saves; attacks against you have Advantage.
> >
> > **Unconscious** — Incapacitated and Prone; you drop what you hold; Speed 0; attacks against you have Advantage; auto-fail STR/DEX saves; hits from within 5 ft are Critical Hits.
>
> > [!tip|no-t] **⚡ Actions & Combat**
> >
> > **On your turn:** move, take one **Action**, and one **Bonus Action** if a feature gives you one. **Reaction:** one per round. You can also interact with one object for free.
> >
> > **Actions:** Attack · Dash · Disengage · Dodge · Help · Hide · Influence · Magic · Ready · Search · Study · Utilize
> >
> > **Attack** — One attack with a weapon or Unarmed Strike; you can equip or unequip one weapon before or after it.
> >
> > **Dodge** — Attacks against you have Disadvantage (if you can see the attacker) and you have Advantage on DEX saves, until your next turn.
> >
> > **Help** — Give an ally Advantage on their next check, or on their next attack against an enemy within 5 ft of you.
> >
> > **Hide** — DC 15 DEX (Stealth) while Heavily Obscured or behind ¾/total cover. Success: you're Invisible until found, you attack, or you cast with a Verbal component.
> >
> > **Influence** — Sway a monster through roleplay; the GM decides whether it's Willing, Unwilling or Hesitant (a check, default DC 15).
> >
> > **Search** — WIS check (Insight, Medicine, Perception, Survival). **Study** — INT check (Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, Religion).
> >
> > ---
> >
> > **Opportunity Attack** — Reaction: one melee attack when a creature you can see leaves your reach.
> >
> > **Two-Weapon Fighting** — After attacking with a **Light** weapon, make one extra attack as a Bonus Action with a different Light weapon (no ability modifier to damage unless negative). The **Nick** mastery lets that extra attack be part of the Attack action.
> >
> > **Grapple** — Unarmed Strike option. Target (no more than one size larger) makes a STR or DEX save (its choice) or is Grappled. You need a free hand.
> >
> > **Shove** — Unarmed Strike option. Target makes a STR or DEX save (its choice) or is pushed 5 ft or knocked Prone.
> >
> > *Save DC for Grapple/Shove = 8 + STR modifier + Proficiency Bonus.*
> >
> > → **Weapon Mastery** reference is below.
>
> > [!abstract|no-t] **🎯 Key DCs & Rules**
> >
> > **Common DCs**
> > | Task Difficulty | DC |
> > |---|:---:|
> > | Very Easy | 5 |
> > | Easy | 10 |
> > | Medium | 15 |
> > | Hard | 20 |
> > | Very Hard | 25 |
> > | Nearly Impossible | 30 |
> >
> > **Advantage / Disadvantage** — Roll two d20s and take the higher / lower. They don't stack and cancel each other out.
> >
> > ---
> >
> > **Death Saves**
> > Roll a d20 on your turn at 0 HP. **10+** = success; **9 or lower** = failure. Natural **20**: regain 1 HP. Natural **1**: 2 failures. 3 successes = stable; 3 failures = dead.
> >
> > Damage at 0 HP = 1 failure (a Critical Hit = 2). Healing ends death saves.
> >
> > ---
> >
> > **Concentration**
> > When you take damage: CON save, DC = 10 or half the damage (rounded down), whichever is higher, max 30. It also ends if you're Incapacitated or die, or start another Concentration effect.
> >
> > ---
> >
> > **Cover**
> > **Half (+2 AC and DEX saves)** — **Three-Quarters (+5)** — **Total** — can't be targeted directly. Only the best applies.
> >
> > **Surprise** — A surprised creature has Disadvantage on its Initiative roll.
> >
> > **Heroic Inspiration** — Spend it to reroll any one die right after rolling, and use the new result.
> >
> > ---
> >
> > **Short Rest** — 1+ hour; spend Hit Dice to heal.
> > **Long Rest** — 8+ hours; regain all HP and all spent Hit Dice, and lose 1 Exhaustion level. 16 hours between Long Rests.
